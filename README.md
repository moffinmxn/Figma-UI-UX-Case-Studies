### 1. SeatSync
**Role:** UI/UX Designer (End-to-End)
**Tools:** Figma
**Overview:** A real-time transit app and hardware system built to solve overcrowding, long queues, and unpredictable commutes in Metro Manila. Guided by its tagline, "Know your seats, go with ease," it connects commuters and Public Utility Vehicle (PUV) drivers with live transportation data to make daily travel faster and more efficient.

Key Highlights
• How It Works: Uses overhead heat sensors, seat sensor pads, and GPS to monitor live seat availability and vehicle locations.
• For Commuters: Helps students, workers, seniors, PWDs, and tourists save time and money through route optimization, smart fare estimation, and accessibility assistance.
• For Drivers: Provides PUV drivers and operators with real-time insights to optimize routes and maximize daily efficiency.

### 2. AI Diet Consultant
**Role:** UI/UX Designer (End-to-End)
**Tools:** Figma
**Overview:** A mobile wellness app that combines daily habit tracking, meal scheduling, and real-time AI coaching to simplify healthy living. Based on your UI wireframes, it serves as an all-in-one personal assistant for managing nutrition, fitness, and daily routines.

Key Highlights
• AI Nutrition Coach: Features an interactive chat assistant ("What can I help you with today?") with attachment support for instant dietary advice and meal logging.
• Daily Wellness Tracking: Monitors weekly progress, step counts, water intake, and calorie ranges across Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner on a clean dashboard.
• Structured Scheduling: Uses daily time-blocks and monthly calendars to help users plan meals, fitness routines, and personal tasks in advance.
